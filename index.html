Work with me

I take on a small number of projects at a time, which means you deal with me directly from first call to launch — no account manager, no handover.

  • New websites

    A site built from scratch for a business that doesn’t have one, or has one it’s embarrassed by.

  • Rebuilds

    An existing site that’s slow, awkward on a phone, or impossible to update without calling someone.

  • Ongoing changes

    Small, regular work on a site that already exists — new pages, fixes, updates.

How it works

  1. A call, freeTwenty minutes on what you need and whether I’m the right person. If I’m not, I’ll say so.

  2. A fixed quoteOne price, one timeline, written down. It doesn’t move unless you change what you asked for.

  3. Build and reviewYou see it working early and often, not once at the end when it’s too late to change.

  4. LaunchIt goes live, and I show you how to make the changes you’ll want to make yourself.
